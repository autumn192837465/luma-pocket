相機與內容

開啟掃描分頁並允許相機權限後，本 App 直接使用相機辨識 QR Code。你也可以透過系統選取器選擇照片或截圖。相機與照片辨識均在裝置上進行，本 App 不建立永久照片副本。Luma Pocket 不接收相機影像、照片、掃描結果與你輸入的文字。

Android 版使用 Google ML Kit 條碼掃描，辨識模型內建於本 App 並在裝置上執行，相機影像與 QR 內容不會傳送給 Google。依 Google 說明，ML Kit 可能傳送診斷與使用統計資訊給 Google，例如裝置型號、作業系統版本、App 套件名稱與版本、功能設定及效能數據，用於維護與改善 ML Kit；這些資訊不包含相機影像或 QR 內容。詳見 ML Kit 資料揭露說明。

本機資料與分享

本 App 會將最近 100 筆掃描、照片辨識與製作結果，以及來源和時間保存在裝置上。相同內容會更新至最上方。你可以在「紀錄」搜尋、刪除單筆或清空紀錄。資料可能隨 Android 或 iOS 的系統備份保存，依你的系統設定而定。當你分享 QR Code 時，本 App 會在裝置上建立暫存 PNG。系統可清除暫存資料。你選擇儲存的圖片由你管理。

當你操作系統分享選單時，圖片會交給你指定的 App 或服務。網址不會自動開啟；按下「開啟網站」後，網址會交由外部瀏覽器處理。接收方及網站的後續處理適用各自政策。本 App 不提供惡意網站偵測或安全保證。

商店更新與評分

  • Android 版：開啟本 App 時，會透過 Google Play 的應用程式內更新功能檢查是否有新版本；製作 QR Code 後，可能呼叫 Google Play 的應用程式內評論介面。這些功能由裝置上的 Google Play 商店處理，適用 Google 隱私權政策。
  • iOS 版：開啟本 App 時會連線至 Apple 商店服務，以 App 識別碼及查詢地區取得公開版本資訊；查詢地區依裝置地區設定，未提供時使用台灣。Apple 會接收連線所需資訊，例如 IP 位址。製作 QR Code 後，本 App 可能呼叫 Apple 的官方評分介面，評分與評論由 Apple 處理。

上述查詢不包含相機影像、照片、QR 內容或歷史紀錄。評分視窗是否顯示由系統決定，本 App 不取得使用者實際給予的星等，也不記錄「已完成評分」。本機僅保存更新提示與評分邀請嘗試的版本、時間，以減少重複提示。

帳號、廣告與分析

本 App 不提供帳號、不含廣告，亦未整合第三方使用者追蹤或分析 SDK。Luma Pocket 不從本 App 蒐集、儲存或分享可識別個人的資料，也不出售任何資料。

權限管理

本 App 使用相機權限掃描 QR Code，並使用網路權限進行上述商店版本檢查。你可以在 Android 或 iOS 設定中撤回相機權限，撤回後仍可使用 QR Code 製作功能。

兒童隱私

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不蒐集兒童的個人資料。

The App is developed and provided by Luma Pocket, which is responsible for the data handling described in this policy.

Camera and photos

QR codes are recognized on your device, from the camera or from photos and screenshots you pick with the system picker. The App does not keep permanent copies of photos. Camera frames, photos, scan results and text you enter are never sent to Luma Pocket.

On Android, scanning uses the Google ML Kit barcode model bundled with the App and running on the device; camera images and QR content are not sent to Google. According to Google, ML Kit may send diagnostic and usage information to Google, such as device model, OS version, app package name and version, API configuration and performance metrics, to maintain and improve ML Kit. This information does not include camera images or QR content. See the ML Kit data disclosure.

On-device history and sharing

The App stores your 100 most recent scan and creation results, with their source and time, only on your device. They may be included in your Android or iOS system backup depending on your settings. You can search, delete single items or clear the history at any time. When you share a QR code, the App creates a temporary PNG file on the device. Images you send through the system share sheet go to the app or service you choose, under that recipient's policy. Links are never opened automatically; they open in your browser only when you tap "Open Website". The App does not check websites for safety.

Store updates and reviews

  • Android: the App uses Google Play In-App Updates to check for new versions and may show the Google Play In-App Review prompt after you create a QR code. These are handled by the Google Play Store app under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • iOS: the App queries Apple's public App Store lookup service with the app identifier and region, and Apple receives connection information such as your IP address. The App may show Apple's rating prompt, which is handled by Apple.

These requests never include camera images, photos, QR content or history. The App never learns the rating you give. It only stores on the device the version and time of update and rating prompts, to avoid repeating them.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The App has no accounts, no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Luma Pocket does not collect, store, share or sell personal data from the App.

Permissions

The App uses the camera permission to scan QR codes and internet access for the store update checks above. You can revoke camera access in system settings at any time and still create QR codes.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If this policy changes, this page and its "last updated" date will be updated.
